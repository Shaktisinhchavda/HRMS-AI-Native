# Employee Handbook & HR Policy

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves

**1.1 Annual Leave**
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.66 days per month. Up to 5 days of unused annual leave can be rolled over to the next calendar year. Rollover leave must be used by March 31st of the following year.

**1.2 Sick Leave**
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually. A medical certificate is required for sick leave extending beyond 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not roll over.

**1.3 Parental Leave**
The company provides up to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary caregivers and 4 weeks of fully paid leave for secondary caregivers following the birth or adoption of a child.

## 2. Remote Work and Flexible Hours

**2.1 Remote Work Policy**
The company operates on a hybrid model. Employees are expected to be in the office at least 2 days a week (Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory core days). The remaining 3 days can be worked remotely. Fully remote exceptions must be approved by the department head and HR.

**2.2 Core Hours**
While we offer flexible start and end times, all employees must be online and available during "Core Hours" which are between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local timezone.

## 3. Benefits and Allowances

**3.1 Home Office Stipend**
Employees are eligible for a one-time $500 stipend to set up their home office (desk, chair, monitor). An additional $50/month allowance is provided to cover home internet costs.

**3.2 Health and Wellness**
Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance are provided for the employee and their dependents. The company also offers a $100/month wellness allowance that can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health app subscriptions.

## 4. Performance and Evaluations

**4.1 Annual Reviews**
Performance reviews occur annually in December. Goal setting for the upcoming year takes place in January.

**4.2 Promotion Cycles**
Promotions and salary adjustments are considered twice a year, at the end of Q2 (July) and Q4 (January). 

## 5. Code of Conduct

**5.1 Anti-Harassment**
The company has a zero-tolerance policy towards harassment or discrimination of any kind. Any violations should be reported immediately to HR or anonymously via our internal reporting hotline.

**5.2 Expense Policy**
Business-related travel and meal expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the receipt date. Meals during business travel are capped at $75 per day per person. Alcohol is not expensable unless part of an approved client dinner.
